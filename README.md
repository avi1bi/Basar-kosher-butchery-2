# Basar Kosher Butchery

Premium responsive storefront prototype with a 62-item beef, lamb and specialty catalog.

## Preview locally
Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step or dependencies are required.

## Included
- 24 beef cuts
- 20 lamb cuts
- 9 beef organ and specialty cuts
- 9 lamb organ and specialty cuts
- Category filters, text search, and mobile-friendly layout
- Browser-local inquiry bag (demonstration only)
- Coming Soon sections for spices and fine kosher wines

## Before launch
- Replace product image placeholders with appropriately licensed, accurate photos of each cut.
- Confirm prices, pack sizes, availability and weight-based selling logic.
- Verify kosher supervision and eligibility of each product, including nikkur.
- Implement secure checkout, payment processing, inventory, fulfillment, tax and shipping rules.
- Add legal policies, contact information, accessibility QA, and end-to-end order testing.

**Important:** This is a storefront prototype, not a production-ready ecommerce system. No orders are transmitted or paid for.
